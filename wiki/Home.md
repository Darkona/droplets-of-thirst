# Droplets of Thirst

Droplets of Thirst adds a thirst bar and water purity to Minecraft. Activity and heat make you thirsty, and the water you find can make you sick: swamp or sea water is unsafe until you clean it. Every number is in a config file or a datapack, so modpacks can tune it. It continues [Thirst Was Taken](https://github.com/ghen-git/Thirst-Mod) by ghen.

## Versions

Both versions have the same thirst, water purity, effects, config files and commands. They differ in the loader, the data formats and the mods they work with. 1.21.1 works with the most mods, and [Compatibility](Compatibility) marks what 1.20.1 lacks.

| Minecraft | Loader | Branch |
|---|---|---|
| [1.21.1](Minecraft-1.21.1) (this wiki) | NeoForge 21.1.252 or newer | `1.21.1` |
| [1.20.1](Minecraft-1.20.1) | Forge 47.1.3 or newer, and NeoForge 47.1 (one jar) | `1.20.1` |

The [GitHub repository](https://github.com/Darkona/droplets-of-thirst) has the downloads, the changelog and the source code.
